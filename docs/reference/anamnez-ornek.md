# Example intake form (anamnez)

`anamnez-ornek-2021.jpg` is a real, blank "Kişisel Bilgi Formu" (dated 2021) that Can
shared on 23 Sep 2026 as an example anamnez. It is **sports-oriented**; whether it is
meant for every client or only athletes is open (QUESTIONS.md K2). Until then, treat it
as an example, not as our form.

What it asks, in its own order:

**Identity:** name, date and place of birth, where they live, whether they do sport (and
for how many years).

1. Regular activities (weekend outings, eating out, cinema…)
2. Household size
3. Serious illness in the family or themselves
4. Regular medication: which, who recommended it, how often and why
5. Supplements / vitamins-minerals: which, when (before/during/after sport), who recommended
6. Smoking (frequency)
7. Alcohol
8. Weight-loss methods used (sauna, diet, fluid restriction, long fasting, exercising in
   heavy clothes) — and, if used: age of first weight cut, how many days before a
   competition and how many kg, how many times so far, who supported it, supplements used
9. Hours of sleep (3–4 / 4–6 / 6–8 / 8–10 / 10–12)
10. Skipped meals, and which
11. Snacking between meals
12. Foods avoided before / during / after training or a match
13. Disliked foods; allergies
14. Foods specifically eaten before / during / after exercise
15. Nutrition support received before, and from whom (doctor, coach, conditioning coach,
    masseur, physiotherapist, dietitian, other)
16. Foods they think affect their performance

Compared with the demo's invented intake form (`intake_form_screen.dart`): this one has
no questions on water intake, bowel habits or blood tests, and adds the whole
weight-cutting and training-food block. Replacing the demo form waits on K2.
